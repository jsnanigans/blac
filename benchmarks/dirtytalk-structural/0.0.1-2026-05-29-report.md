## Scenarios

- `patch` — StructuralContainer.patch() no-diff path (500,000 patches)
- `emit-diff` — StructuralContainer.emit() diff-along-skeleton, 50 consumers (20,000 emits)
- `track-render` — trackRender proxy recording of nested reads (200,000 renders)
- `intern` — PathInterner steady-state interning, 1000-path vocabulary (2,000,000 interns)
